Video 1 — Steinsel discharge under six weather-model forcings

Synchronized six-panel comparison of simulated water depth and observed/simulated discharge at Steinsel for the July 2021 Alzette flood, Luxembourg. Forcings are WRF 1.3 km after data assimilation, WRF 12 km after data assimilation, WRF 12 km before data assimilation, GraphCast, AIFS and FuXi; LISFLOOD-FP hydraulic settings are identical across runs. Maps and the hydrograph time marker advance from 13 July 2021 18:00 UTC to 17 July 2021 18:00 UTC in six-hour steps. Maps display maximum depth in 40 m blocks from 10 m cells; depths below 0.10 m are hidden. They include river channels and have not been validated against observed flood extent. No intermediate flood states are interpolated. AIFS training includes this event.

Video 2 — Walferdange observed stage and simulated depth under six weather-model forcings

Synchronized six-panel comparison of simulated water-depth maps and observed stage/simulated cell water depth at Walferdange for the July 2021 Alzette flood, Luxembourg. The six forcings, hydraulic settings, map display and time window are as described for Video 1. The red map marker locates Walferdange. Observed stage and simulated depth are not calibrated to a common vertical datum; their vertical offset must not be interpreted as an absolute water-level error. Hydrograph shape and peak timing are the relevant diagnostics. Maps are not validated against observed flood extent. AIFS training includes this event.
